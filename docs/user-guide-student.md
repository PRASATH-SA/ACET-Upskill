# SkillUp student guide

SkillUp turns your syllabus into a map of connected topics. Learn each topic in six short steps,
practise, and let revision bring topics back just before you would forget them.

## Signing in

Use your college email and the password your HOD or department gave you. Change a temporary
password after your first sign-in (**Profile**). Forgot it? Ask your HOD or department admin.

On a phone, open SkillUp in Chrome and choose **Install app** (or *Add to Home screen*). It then
opens like an app, and pages you have already opened work even without internet. A banner tells
you when you are offline; your revision ratings are sent when you are back online.

## Learn

**Learn** shows your subjects. Open one to see the **learning path**: topics in the order to study
them, one unit at a time.

| Topic colour | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Gold | Mastered |
| Blue (glowing) | Your current topic |
| White with a blue ring | Ready to start |
| Grey | Locked: learn its prerequisites first |
| Gold with an orange dot | Mastered, but due for revision |

Tap **Map** to see the concept map: solid lines are prerequisites, dashed lines are related
topics. Tap a line to see why two topics are connected.

## A topic page: six steps

1. **Curiosity:** a real-life question to get you thinking. Pick an answer, then reveal it.
2. **Understand:** a short video and an easy explanation.
3. **Prepare:** exam study material for the topic — notes for short and long answers, quick questions with answers, and a print button.
4. **Remember:** flashcards. Tap to flip; say whether you knew it.
5. **Practice:** a quiz with an explanation for every option, and **Code lab** SQL problems.
6. **Apply:** where it is used in real life, and workshop tasks from your faculty.

The right side shows connected topics and **previous-year exam questions** for the topic.

**Ask why** opens the tutor. It answers from your faculty's material and shows where each answer
comes from. It only answers questions about the topic.

## Code lab (SQL practice)

Open **Code lab** from the menu, or a topic's Practice step.

1. Read the problem; open **Tables** to see the columns and sample rows.
2. Write SQL in the editor. **Run** (or Ctrl+Enter) shows your result on the sample data.
3. **Submit** (or Ctrl+Shift+Enter) checks it against hidden tests too. A wrong answer shows the
   expected output next to yours.
4. Stuck? Open **Hints**, one at a time.

Column names don't matter, only values. Row order matters only when the problem asks for it.

## Quiz, revision and mastery

- Score 80% or more in a topic quiz to move it towards **mastered**.
- After a quiz, the topic's flashcards and the questions you missed go into **Revision**.
- **Revision** shows what is due today. Rate each card honestly: *Again, Hard, Good* or *Easy*.
  Easy cards come back later; hard ones come back sooner.
- A mastered topic you don't revise turns to *needs revision* (gold with an orange dot).

## Exam prep and progress

**Exam prep** lists previous-year questions by topic, most asked first, with filters for unit and
marks. **Progress** shows mastery per subject, your revision streak and your weakest topics.

Only you (and your mentor and faculty) can see your progress.
